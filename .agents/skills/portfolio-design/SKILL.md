# Design the portfolio

- Keep the first screen clear about who Paul is, what he builds, and where to find the work. Treat the mushroom workshop as an illustration, not navigation.
- Start each case study with the problem, Paul's role and contribution, and the outcome. Distinguish team results from individual work; only use claims and assets supplied or verified by the owner. Mark missing evidence in the plan, not in the published copy.
- Give CHRIMS the most space; use game art and hardware imagery for Ultima Spes and CLOTHGENT when provided. Give each image a job rather than filling space.
- Reuse the existing dark palette and semantic HTML. Check reading order, contrast, visible focus, and narrow layouts before adding motion. For choreography, use `/portfolio-motion`.
